# Translation guide

## Sources of truth

The canonical philosophical source is:

- `docs/zh-CN/README.md`

The canonical public voice and landing-page structure are:

- `README.md`

The formal definitions are:

- `docs/zh-CN/FORMAL_SPEC.md`

The canonical terminology and aliases are:

- `docs/zh-CN/GLOSSARY.md`

Translations should reproduce the reading effect, not Chinese sentence order:
clear, patient, concise, and natural in the target language. Avoid importing
aggressive slang or a lecturing tone. They must not strengthen a metaphysical
axiom into an established scientific claim.

## Required distinctions

Every translation must preserve the separation between definitions, logical
judgments, experience models, practical methods, philosophical axioms, and
reality or safety boundaries.

It must also preserve these qualifications:

- the reader-facing argument keeps the P1–P5 order used by the canonical guide;
- probability alone does not prove that all possibilities are realized;
- possibility and probability are not interchangeable terms;
- first-person branch convergence is an independent axiom, not a conclusion
  derived from the first four propositions;
- goals and even higher-level directions may be honestly revised as knowledge,
  facts, or ethical judgment change; a new goal does not retroactively complete
  an abandoned one;
- local actions and consequences remain real;
- everyday examples explain the framework and are not evidence for it.

## Formatting

- Keep Markdown headings and tables valid.
- Keep formulas and Mermaid identifiers unchanged when possible.
- Use native punctuation and readable local prose.
- Link back to `../../README.md` from each locale guide.
- Supporting documents currently exist only in `zh-CN`; locale guides must link
  to `../zh-CN/FORMAL_SPEC.md` and `../zh-CN/CRITICISM.md` instead of creating
  broken local links.
- End each guide with a translation status note.
